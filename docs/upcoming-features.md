# Upcoming Features

## Daily Lotto Results

Track and display daily lottery results with historical data and notifications.

**Planned Features:**
- Daily lottery result tracking and display
- Historical data archive
- Notification system for result updates
- Search and filter functionality

## Helper Assistant

Manage domestic helper records including attendance tracking, leave balance, monthly salary, and employment history.

**Planned Features:**
- Attendance tracking with daily logs
- Leave balance management (days off remaining)
- Monthly salary calculation and records
- Contract and Personal details
- Payment schedule tracking
- Submitted requirements

## Tenant Assistant

Comprehensive rental management with contract storage, invoice generation, payment tracking, tenant contact information, and lease terms.

**Planned Features:**
- Digital contract storage and management
- Automated invoice generation
- Monthly payment tracking
- Down payment records
- Tenant contact information database
- Lease term and renewal management
- Payment history and status
